# Market Research Lab: Task 1 (Google Trends)

Part D of Module 2 (Marketing Environment): the organisation wants to promote digital-skills
programmes to university students in Nigeria, and Task 1 compares online search interest in the
five programme areas on Google Trends.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| [Task1_Google_Trends.docx](Task1_Google_Trends.docx) | The Task 1 write-up, ready to copy into the Module 2 document. Opens in Word 2013 and later. |
| [src/build_task1.js](src/build_task1.js) | The script that builds it (`npm install` in `src/`, then `node build_task1.js ../Task1_Google_Trends.docx`). |

Google Trends (trends.google.com) was blocked by the network policy of the environment that
built the file, so it contains no search figures. The objective, method, search terms, time
period and location are written in. Every result is a yellow blank inside a ready-made
sentence or table. The last page links to the prepared Google Trends comparison (Nigeria, past
12 months and past 5 years) and says where each answer is found.
