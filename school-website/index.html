About Our School

Brightstar Primary School is a caring and friendly school where every child is helped to reach their full potential. Our dedicated teachers make learning fun and meaningful, and our classrooms are full of curiosity, creativity and kindness.

We run five classes, from Primary 1 to Primary 5. Each class has a class teacher who guides the pupils through a broad curriculum that includes English, Mathematics, Science, Social Studies, Computer Studies and the Creative Arts.
