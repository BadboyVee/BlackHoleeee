About the Class

Primary 1 is where our pupils take their first big steps in formal learning. The class focuses on reading, writing and counting through songs, stories, games and lots of hands-on activities, so every child feels confident and happy at school.

Our Subjects

  • English Language – Phonics, letter sounds, simple words and short sentences.
  • Mathematics – Counting to 100, simple addition and subtraction, shapes.
  • Basic Science – Our body, plants, animals and the world around us.
  • Social Studies – My family, my school and my community.
  • Creative Arts – Drawing, colouring, music and rhymes.
  • Physical & Health Education – Games, movement and good hygiene habits.
