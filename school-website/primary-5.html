About the Class

Primary 5 prepares pupils for their final year and for secondary school. The class develops critical thinking, problem solving and leadership. Pupils take on class responsibilities, lead projects and get ready for common entrance examinations.

Our Subjects

  • English Language – Essay writing, comprehension, literature and oral English.
  • Mathematics – Percentages, ratio, algebra basics and data handling.
  • Basic Science & Technology – Electricity, the human body systems and the environment.
  • Social Studies – Government, history and world geography.
  • Computer Studies – Spreadsheets, presentations and simple coding.
  • Quantitative & Verbal Reasoning – Practice for entrance examinations.
