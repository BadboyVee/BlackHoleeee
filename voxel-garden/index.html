天空の庭

Tenkū no Niwa · a garden in the sky

What can I do here?

  • 🖱Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag to pan.
  • 🐉Click the dragon to make it roar, breathe fire and barrel-roll.
  • 🏯Click each pagoda roof: every tier is a note, and the bells answer.
  • 🧑Click a villager to say hello. They sweep, pray, fish, sip tea and feed the koi.
  • 🐟Click the pond to scatter food and watch the koi come.
  • 🔔Ring the temple bell; birds scatter from the trees.
  • 🏮Click stone lanterns to light or snuff them.
  • 🎋Tap the shishi-odoshi, shake a tree, toss a coin at the shrine and send a lantern into the sky.
  • 🪨Drag across the sand in the rock garden to rake new patterns.
  • 🌸Switch seasons and slide the time of day at the bottom.

Keys: 1–6 camera views · Space pause time · M sound · F fullscreen · H help
