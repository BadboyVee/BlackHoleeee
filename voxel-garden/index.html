    Welcome to Kumoniwa

    A small temple garden drifts above a sea of clouds. Villagers keep it, a dragon keeps watch, and nearly everything answers when you click it.

    Look around

    • Drag to orbit, right-drag to pan
    • Scroll or pinch to zoom
    • V viewpoints · D ride the dragon
    • Esc back to the free camera

    Change the world

    • Space pause or run the day
    • 1–4 spring, summer, autumn, winter
    • R rain · T miniature lens · M sound

    Things to try

    • Ring the bronze bell beside the pagoda, then watch the sky.
    • Click the pagoda to open it up storey by storey.
    • Click the pond to feed the koi. Shake a tree. Light a lantern at night.
    • Drag across the white gravel garden to rake it.
    • Talk to the villagers, bow to the deer, wake the cat.
    • At night, click the sky.
