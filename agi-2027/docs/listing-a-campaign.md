# Listing a campaign on AGI 2027

This guide is for you, the owner. It explains how to put a campaign on the site, field by field.
It also covers what happens after: the drop, claiming, drafts, proof, review, payment and edits.

Read it once from top to bottom. After that, use the headings to jump to what you need.

> **Today the admin is a preview.** Every screen works with demo data, but nothing is saved yet.
> Saving arrives in Milestone 5. The last section lists what is real today and what comes when.

---

## The short version

1. Click **New campaign** (top of the sidebar, or the round button on your phone).
2. Fill six short steps: **Brand and basics**, **Brief**, **Requirements**, **Payout and audience**,
   **Timing**, **Internal and review**.
3. On the last step, check the summary and the warnings.
4. Press the final button. It says **Publish now**, **Schedule drop** or **Save draft**, depending
   on what you picked.
5. Creators in the audience see it on their board, get a notification and an email, and the
   Telegram bot posts it in the creators group.
6. Creators claim. You give each one an angle and a post time from the campaign's roster.
7. Creators post and paste the link as proof. Free checks run. You approve.
8. Each approval creates an Unpaid earning. You mark it paid with a reference.

The builder saves as you type, on this device. You can close the tab and come back later.

---

## Before you start

Have these ready. It takes about five minutes with them, and much longer without them.

- **The brand:** its name, website and X handle. A logo if it's new to us.
- **The brief:** what the company does, what the post should say, and the one key message.
- **The rules:** accounts to tag, hashtags, words that must appear, and any links.
- **The money:** what each post pays, in which currency, and how many creators you want.
- **The times:** when it drops, when people post, and when proof is due.
- **The client's budget**, so the builder can check the payouts against it.

## Where to start

There are four ways in:

- **New campaign** at the top of the sidebar, on a computer.
- The round **New campaign** button above the tab bar, on a phone.
- Press **⌘K** on a Mac or **Ctrl K** on Windows, type "new", and press Enter.
- **Duplicate** an old campaign from its **⋯** menu. Everything is copied except the times.

Duplicating is the fastest way to rerun a campaign. See [Duplicate](#duplicate).

## Step 1: Brand and basics

This step decides who the campaign is for and what kind of post it asks for.

### Brand

**What it is.** The startup the campaign is for.

**What creators see.** The brand's name and logo at the top of the campaign card.

**How to fill it.** Click the field and pick a brand. Type to filter the list. If the brand is new,
pick **Add a new brand** at the bottom. If you typed a name first, it is carried over.

For a new brand, fill in:

- **Brand name** (required). How creators will see it. Example: `Parallax`.
- **Website** (optional). The full address, starting with `https://`.
- **X handle** (optional). With or without the `@`. The `@` is added for you.
- **Logo.** Upload arrives in Milestone 5. Until then the card shows the brand's initials.

The new brand is saved to **Clients** with the campaign, so next time it's one tap.

**Common mistakes.**

- Adding a brand that already exists under a slightly different name. Search first.
- Typing the website without `https://`. The builder asks for the full link.

### Title

**What it is.** The name of the campaign. Up to 80 characters.

**What creators see.** The big line on the campaign card and on the campaign page.

**Good examples.**

- `Nebula 2.0 launch day`
- `Series A announcement`
- `Open-weights model drop`

**Common mistakes.**

- Naming the task instead of the launch. `Quote tweet campaign #4` tells creators nothing.
- Writing a slogan. Clear beats clever.

### One-line summary

**What it is.** One sentence on what creators will post. Between 10 and 140 characters.

**What creators see.** The line under the title on the card. It's often the reason they open it.

**Good examples.**

- `Quote the launch post with your honest first take on Nebula's new agent builder.`
- `A 30 to 60 second clip of the model running locally, with your one-line verdict.`

**Common mistakes.**

- Repeating the title.
- Putting the payout here. The card already shows it.

### Platform

**What it is.** Where the post goes: **X**, **TikTok**, **Instagram**, **LinkedIn** or **YouTube**.

**What creators see.** The platform name and icon on the card.

**Good to know.** X gets the free proof checks (see [Proof and the free checks](#proof-and-the-free-checks)).
Posts on the other platforms are checked by hand for now.

### Deliverable

**What it is.** The kind of post.

| Deliverable | What creators do |
| --- | --- |
| Original post | A new post in their own words. |
| Quote post | Quote a post you choose, with their take on top. |
| Reply | Reply under a post you choose. |
| Thread | Several connected posts that tell a fuller story. |
| Video | A short clip, posted natively. |
| Other | Anything else. Explain it in the brief. |

**What creators see.** "Quote post on X" (or similar) under the brand name on the card.

**Common mistake.** Picking **Other** and not explaining it in the brief. Creators only see the
word "Other".

### Post to quote or reply to

**When it shows.** Only for **Quote post** and **Reply**. It's required for both.

**What it is.** The full link to the post creators should quote or reply to.

**Good example.** `https://x.com/nebula_ai/status/1975000000000000001`

**Common mistakes.**

- Pasting the brand's profile link instead of the post link.
- Pasting a link to a post that isn't up yet. Check it opens before you publish.

The review step warns you if the link doesn't look like an X post link.

### Tracking or referral link

**What it is.** A link every creator must include, like a referral or UTM link. Optional.

**What creators see.** The link in the brief, under "Must include".

**Good example.** `https://nebula.example/?ref=agi2027`

**Common mistake.** Adding a link the brand doesn't track. If nobody reads the numbers, leave it out.

## Step 2: Brief

This is what creators read before they claim. Keep it short and concrete.

### About the company

Two or three sentences: what the company does and who it's for. At least 20 characters.

> Nebula builds AI agents that run inside a company's own tools. Version 2.0 adds a visual
> builder anyone on a team can use.

### What the post should say

What to try, show or react to. Real reactions beat feature lists. At least 20 characters.

> Try the builder for 10 minutes, then quote the launch post with what surprised you.

**Common mistake.** Pasting the client's press release. Creators can't post that, and their
audience can tell.

### Key message

The one line every post must land, whatever the angle. Up to 280 characters.

> Anyone on your team can build a working agent in an afternoon.

### Do's and don'ts

Short rules creators can check their post against. Up to 12 of each, 200 characters each.

- Type a rule and press **Enter**, or tap a suggestion to add it.
- Use the pencil to edit a rule and the bin to remove it.

Good do's: `Use the paid partnership label`, `Post inside your window`, `Write it in your own voice`.

Good don'ts: `Don't promise returns or results`, `Don't tag competitors`.

### Angles

**What they are.** Suggested hooks, so ten posts don't all sound the same. Optional, up to 20.

**What creators see.** The list on the brief, and later the one angle you give them.

**How it works.** Add the angles here. After people claim, open the campaign and pick an angle for
each creator in the roster. They get a notification with their angle.

- Press **Enter** to add one. Use the arrows to reorder. Put your favorite first.

**Good examples.**

- `The 10-minute build: show what you made`
- `Why this matters for non-engineers`
- `The one feature you didn't expect`

The review step warns you if there are no angles. It doesn't stop you.

## Step 3: Requirements

### Mentions, hashtags and keywords

These must appear in every post. The free checks look for them when proof comes in (on X).

Type one and press **Enter** or a comma. Paste a list separated by commas to add them all.
Each tag is checked before it's added. Up to 10 of each.

| Field | Format | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mentions | An X handle: up to 15 letters, numbers or underscores. The `@` is added for you. | `@nebula_ai` |
| Hashtags | Letters, numbers or underscores, no spaces. The `#` is added for you. | `#Nebula2` |
| Keywords | Any word or short phrase, up to 60 characters. | `agent builder` |

**Common mistakes.**

- A hashtag with a space or a dash, like `#launch day`. Use `#LaunchDay`.
- Too many required tags. Three or four is plenty. Posts with ten tags read like ads.

### Paid partnership label

**On by default.** Creators must turn on X's paid partnership label, and tick a box confirming it
when they send proof.

Only turn it off for posts nobody is paid for. The builder warns you when it's off.

### Draft required

**Off by default.** When on, creators send their post as a draft first. You approve it, or ask
for changes with a comment. They post only after you approve.

Turn it on for sensitive launches, new creators, or clients who want to see posts first.
It adds a day or so, so leave enough time before the window.

### Assets

Images, videos and documents creators can download from the brief.

- Drop files on the box, or click it to choose them. Up to 20 files, 200 MB each.
- Each file shows its name, kind (image, video, document) and size. Remove one with the cross.
- **Brand kit link** (optional): a folder with logos, colors and screenshots.

> Today files are listed but not uploaded. Uploads to private storage arrive in Milestone 5.
> Only creators who can see the campaign will get download links.

## Step 4: Payout and audience

### Amount and currency

**What it is.** What each approved post earns. Pick the currency: **USD**, **USDC**, **USDT** or **NGN**.

**Format.** Exact amounts only: `250`, `99.50`, `180000`. No commas, no currency symbols, at most two
decimals. The amount has to be more than 0.

**Good to know.** Money is stored as exact decimals, never rounded. Totals are always kept per
currency. USD and NGN are never added together anywhere.

### Slots

**What it is.** How many creators can take part. From 1 to 1,000. Use the minus and plus buttons,
or type the number.

**What creators see.** "7 of 20 slots left" on the card, with a bar that fills up.

### What the campaign commits you to

Under the amount you'll see the total: amount × slots, in the payout currency.

> 250 USDC × 20 slots. Commits 5,000 USDC.

With custom amounts, it shows a range, from everyone at the lowest amount to everyone at the
highest.

### Custom amount per tier

Optional. Pay a tier more or less than the base amount. Switch a tier on and type its amount.

Example: base 250 USDC, Tier 1 gets 320 USDC.

Each tier can have one custom amount. If a hand-picked creator has their own amount, it beats
their tier's.

### Audience

Who sees the campaign. Everyone else never knows it exists.

| Audience | Who sees it |
| --- | --- |
| All creators | Every approved creator. |
| Chosen tiers | Only creators in the tiers you pick. |
| Chosen tags | Only creators with at least one of the tags you pick. |
| Hand-picked | Only the creators you choose by name. |

The count in the corner shows how many creators can see it right now.

For **Hand-picked**, tick each creator. You can type a custom amount for anyone you pick. Leave
it empty to pay them the base amount.

**Common mistakes.**

- Picking **Chosen tiers** and forgetting to pick a tier. The builder won't go on until you do.
- More slots than hand-picked creators. Some slots can never fill. The builder warns you.

### Claim mode

| Mode | What happens when a creator taps Claim |
| --- | --- |
| Instant | First come, first served. The slot is theirs straight away. |
| Apply | They send a short note. You accept each request from the review queue. |

Use **Instant** for big, fast campaigns. Use **Apply** when you want to choose who takes part.

## Step 5: Timing

### How it goes out

| Choice | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Save as draft | Only your team can see it. Publish or schedule it later. |
| Publish now | It drops the moment you press the final button. |
| Schedule a drop time | It stays private until the time you pick, then drops on its own. |

When editing a campaign that's already out, this choice disappears. Saving updates it in place.

### The four times

| Time | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Drop time | When the campaign appears and notifications go out. Only when scheduling. |
| Post window opens | The earliest anyone can post. |
| Post window closes | The latest anyone can post. |
| Claiming closes | The Claim button closes. |
| Proof is due | The last moment to paste the post link. |

**The rules between them.** The builder checks these for you:

- The window has to close after it opens.
- Claiming has to close before the window closes. Ideally before it opens.
- Proof can't be due before the window closes.
- A scheduled drop has to be in the future, and before claiming closes.
- If you publish now or schedule, claiming must still be open.

**Time zones.** You enter every time in your own time zone. The builder shows the same moment in
UTC under each field. Times are saved in UTC. Each creator sees them in their own time zone, with
UTC beside it, so nobody gets confused across countries.

**Suggest the rest.** Set when the window opens, then press **Suggest the rest** to fill the empty
times with a typical shape: a 4 hour window, claiming closes 2 hours before it, and proof is due a
day after it opens. Change anything you like after.

**The timeline.** Under the fields, a small timeline draws the drop, the claim deadline, the window
and the proof deadline. It updates as you type, and it turns red if something is out of order. Under
it, one sentence tells you how long creators get for each part.

**Per-creator post times.** You don't set these in the builder. After people claim, open the
campaign and set each creator's post time in the roster, inside the window. Spreading posts out
keeps the brand trending for longer.

**Common mistakes.**

- A window under an hour. Creators in other time zones miss it. The builder warns you.
- Proof due right when the window closes. Give people a few hours to paste their link.
- Scheduling the drop minutes before claiming closes. Nobody has time to claim.

## Step 6: Internal and review

### Admin notes

Context for whoever runs the campaign. Creators never see it, and it never appears in client
reports. Up to 4,000 characters.

> Client wants 20 posts inside the first four hours.

### Budget and the budget check

Type the client's budget for creators and pick its currency. The builder compares it with what the
campaign commits you to:

- **Within budget:** how much is left if every slot is paid.
- **Over budget:** by how much, if every slot is paid the highest amount.

If the budget and the payout use different currencies, the builder says so. It never converts
currencies.

### Summary

Every answer, grouped by step. Each group has an **Edit** button that takes you back to that step.
Your answers stay where they are when you jump around.

### Warnings

Soft checks that never block saving, but are worth a second look:

- Paying every slot costs more than the budget.
- The highest custom amount would push it over the budget.
- The budget is in a different currency from the payout.
- More slots than hand-picked creators.
- No angles yet.
- The paid partnership label is off.
- The post window is under an hour.
- The post to quote or reply to doesn't look like an X post link.

### The final button

Its label follows your choice in Timing:

- **Publish now**
- **Schedule drop**
- **Save draft**
- **Save changes** (when editing a campaign that's already out)

If anything is missing or wrong, the builder takes you to the first step with a problem. The
fields that need a look are marked in red, and the stepper shows a red mark on that step.

## What happens when it drops

At the drop time (or straight away with Publish now):

1. The campaign appears on the board for the creators in the audience. Nobody else sees it.
2. Each of them gets an in-app notification and an email.
3. The Telegram bot posts it in the creators group, with a link to claim.
4. The post window shows in each creator's time zone and in UTC.

A scheduled campaign shows as **Scheduled** until then. Only your team can see it.

---

## How claiming works

### Instant

The first creators to tap **Claim** get the slots. When the last slot goes, the campaign shows as
**Full**.

### Apply

Creators send a short note with their request. Requests wait in your review queue. Accept up to the
number of slots. A request doesn't hold a slot until you accept it. Requests still waiting when
claiming closes are declined automatically.

### A campaign can never be overfilled

Slots are counted inside the database, in one step, the moment someone claims. If 40 creators tap
Claim at the same second on a 10 slot campaign, exactly 10 get it and 30 see "Full".

### Dropping out and expiry

- A creator can drop their claim before their post window starts. The slot opens again.
- If proof isn't in by the proof deadline, the claim expires and the slot opens again.
- Each creator can claim a campaign once. You can reinstate someone from the roster.

## Drafts

Only when **Draft required** is on.

1. The creator writes the draft in the portal and can attach media.
2. It lands in your review queue.
3. You approve it, or ask for changes with a comment.
4. They edit and resend until it's approved. Then they post inside their window.

---

## Proof and the free checks

After posting, the creator pastes the post link and ticks a box confirming the paid partnership
label and the brief. A screenshot is optional.

Free checks run on every proof link. No paid X account is needed:

- The link must be a real post link for the campaign's platform.
- On X, the username in the link must match the creator's X handle.
- The same link can't be used twice, across every campaign.
- The post is shown embedded so you can check it fast.
- If the post data loads, the checks also look at the post time (inside the window?) and the
  required tags and link.

A failed check shows as a flag on the review card. It never rejects anything on its own. If the post
can't load, the card says to check it by hand.

---

## Reviewing

Everything waiting on you sits in the **Review queue**, oldest first: claim requests, drafts and
proof links. The count shows in the sidebar and on the Review tab on your phone.

- Approve each item in one tap, or reject it with a reason.
- Rejected proof can be fixed and resent until the proof deadline.
- On the campaign page, tick several rows and use **Approve** in the bar that appears.

---

## Paying creators

1. Approving proof creates an earning for the creator, marked **Unpaid**.
2. Pay them outside the platform, by wallet or bank, as today.
3. In **Payouts**, select one or many earnings and mark them **Paid** with the date, amount,
   method and reference (a transaction hash or bank reference).
4. The creator sees it as **Paid** with the reference.

Each claim keeps the payout it was claimed at. Editing the campaign later never changes what someone
already signed up for.

## After it's live

### Editing a live campaign

Open the campaign and press **Edit**. The same six steps open with everything filled in. Saving
updates it in place, and creators on it get a notification that the brief changed.

### Duplicate

From the **⋯** menu on the list or the campaign page. The builder opens with the brand, brief, rules,
payout and audience copied. The times are cleared, so you pick new ones on the Timing step.

### Pause and resume

**Pause** hides it from new claims. Creators already on it carry on. **Resume** opens it again.

### Close

No new claims, for good. Claims in progress carry on until they're settled.

### Mark complete

Once every claim is paid, expired or cancelled. The campaign moves to **Completed**.

### Campaign statuses

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Draft | Only the agency can see it. |
| Scheduled | Drops automatically at the scheduled time. |
| Live | Open to creators now. |
| Paused | Closed to new claims for now. |
| Full | Every slot is taken. A slot reopens if someone drops out. |
| Closed | No new claims. Claims in progress carry on. |
| Completed | Every claim on this campaign is settled. |

## Your work is saved as you type

- The builder saves your answers on this device half a second after each change. The header says
  when it last saved.
- Close the tab and come back: you pick up where you left off, on the same step.
- **Start over** clears the form and the saved copy. It asks first.
- Edits to an existing campaign are saved separately, so they never overwrite a new one.
- Duplicating starts from the campaign you picked, not from an unsaved new campaign.

This copy lives in this browser only. From Milestone 5, drafts are saved to your account.
